Privacy Policy

Hanat · Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Hanat ("the App"), published by Tahmid Rahman ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

The App has no user accounts, no login and no server of our own that receives information about you. Prayer times are calculated on your device. We ourselves do not collect, sell or share personal information. The App shows one banner advertisement, and the advertising service collects some device information, as described below.

Location

The App needs to know where you are to calculate prayer times and to list nearby mosques. You can either let it read your device's location or choose your city by hand.

  • Approximate location is requested if you tap "Use my location" during setup. It is used on your device to find your city and is then stored on your device only.
  • Precise location is requested only when you open the mosque list, so that distances are meaningful. It is used on your device to measure and sort distances and is not stored.
  • The App reads your location only while it is open. It never tracks your location in the background.

Your coordinates are never sent to us.

Mosque list

To show nearby mosques, the App downloads a data file covering the area you are in (a region roughly 55 km across) from our file host, GitHub Pages. As with any download, the host receives your device's IP address and the name of the file requested, from which a region, but not your position, can be inferred. GitHub's handling of such requests is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Downloaded files are kept on your device so the list works offline.

The mosque data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors.

Adding or reporting a mosque

If you choose "Add it" to add a missing mosque, or report a mosque's name or place as wrong, the App sends a public, anonymous note to OpenStreetMap so volunteers can check it. The note contains only the mosque's position, the name you typed (if any), your answer about a women's prayer area, and a fixed description. It contains nothing about you, but anyone can read it, and OpenStreetMap receives your device's IP address with the request, as described in the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. A copy of mosques you added is kept on your device, so the list can show them as "waiting to be added" until they appear in the data.

While you place the pin, the map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which receive your IP address and the area being shown.

Maps and directions

If you tap "Start navigation" or "Find more in Maps", the App opens the maps application installed on your device and passes it the mosque's position or a search near your position. What that application does with the information is governed by its own privacy policy.

Notifications and alarms

The App schedules prayer alerts on your device and, if you allow it, shows notifications and plays the adhan. This happens entirely on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your chosen city, language, alert settings, calculation settings and downloaded mosque files are stored on your device. Uninstalling the App removes them. If your device has backup switched on, Android may include app settings in your own device backup.

Advertising

The App displays one banner advertisement on its home screen, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain device information, including a mobile advertising identifier (Advertising ID), general usage and diagnostic data, and approximate, non-precise location derived from that data. This processing is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from partner sites/apps
  • Google Privacy Policy

The App does not pass the location you gave it for prayer times, or your precise location, to the advertising service. Where the law requires your consent for personalised advertising, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later from that form.

We have configured AdMob to request only G-rated (general audiences) ad content. You can reset or limit your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Analytics

We use no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

Children

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
